Accuracy notes. Snapshots are The Odds API's 5-minute captures — real bookmaker prices with a per-book last_update, but sampled: moves between grid points are invisible, and a book whose last_update is old inside a snapshot is showing a stale price. The scanner below treats staleness explicitly. Historical endpoints require a paid The Odds API plan; error messages from the API are shown verbatim.

4 · Cross-book scan, snapshot by snapshot

For every snapshot: best price per outcome across books → implied total. Totals under 100% are candidate arbitrages. The freshness filter drops books whose last_update lags the snapshot — the honest filter that kills most phantom “arbs”.
