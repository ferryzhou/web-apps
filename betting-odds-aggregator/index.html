Honesty notes.
  • Timestamp precision: sportsbook pages and prediction-market prices move continuously; quotes republished by media articles are only precise to the publication day. The “window” column states the precision claimed — nothing sharper.
  • Media-quoted lines (FOX, ESPN) report a book’s price at writing time and may lag the live line.
  • Kalshi prices are last-trade cents per $1 contract; they embed no bookmaker margin but do carry trading fees (≈ 7% × price × (1 − price) per contract), modeled by the fee toggle above.
  • Theoretical vs. executable: a cross-source “arbitrage” shown here ignores bet limits, account restrictions, line movement between placing legs, and settlement-rule differences. That gap is the point of the exercise.
  • This page is educational analysis of historical data, not betting advice.
