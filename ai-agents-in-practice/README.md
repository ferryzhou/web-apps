# AI Agents in Practice

Short notes from 8 interviews in 2026 with founders and engineering leaders who run AI agents in real companies: Amol Avasare (Anthropic), Boris Cherny (Claude Code), Bret Taylor (Sierra), Guillermo Rauch (Vercel), Jason Lemkin (SaaStr), Nan Yu & Jacob Shumway (Linear), Ryan Lopopolo (OpenAI) and Tobi Lütke (Shopify).

Each summary links to the original video and lists what the guest does with agents and what they learned. Numbers are the guests' own claims.

Single self-contained `index.html`, no build step. Use the list or the arrow keys to move between interviews; each one has its own `#anchor` link.
