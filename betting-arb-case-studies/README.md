# Betting Market Arbitrage — Case Studies & Prior Art

Two first-hand case studies built from real timestamped odds data in this repo,
plus the published record: academic papers (1990–2026), the Kaunitz "beat the
bookies" experiment, UK Gambling Commission account-restriction statistics, the
commercial arb industry, and the value-betting syndicates. Every figure links to
its source; unverifiable items are flagged in-page.

Open `index.html` — static content, no build step.
