# 光影与救赎 · 基督徒影评辑录

A curated, single-page anthology of **Christian film criticism** in Chinese —
gathering film readings by four Chinese Christian writers and scholars:

- **王书亚（王怡）** — 18 essays reading films through *sin · grace · redemption · hope*
  (from *《天堂沉默了半小时》* and other collections)
- **石衡潭** — “透过电影看人生”, viewing cinema from a philosophical + biblical height
  (*《电影之于人生》* series)
- **刘小枫** — an *narrative-ethics* reading of Kieślowski’s *Dekalog* and the
  *Three Colours* trilogy (from *《沉重的肉身》*)
- **齐宏伟** — discerning “沉沦与救赎” (fall and redemption) in film and news

## Design notes

- Single self-contained `index.html` — no build step, no external requests.
- Each film is shown as a **typographic CSS “poster” card** (no copyrighted poster
  images are used). Click a card to open its review.
- Warm, book-like serif aesthetic; responsive down to mobile.

## ⚠️ On copyright & sourcing

Every review’s “core viewpoint” is an **original paraphrase / summary** written by
the editor — **not a verbatim reproduction** of any author’s text. Copyright to the
original essays belongs to their respective authors. Each film links out to the
**original source** so readers can (and should) read the authors’ own writing in full.

If an author or rights-holder would like an entry adjusted or removed, please open
an issue on the repository.
