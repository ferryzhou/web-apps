Cross-Venue Spread Monitor

Watches the same asset's live price on multiple venues using free public APIs, straight from your browser — no server, no keys. It computes the executable cross: highest bid minus lowest ask, net of taker fees, only across venues whose quotes are fresh. Crypto is the default domain because its public APIs are genuinely free and open; the same framework applies to any market. Siblings: strategy lab · betting case studies · in-play odds explorer.

Monitor

Idle. “Age” is seconds since that venue's quote was received; quotes older than 3 polls are marked stale and excluded from the cross. Venues that error back off and retry automatically.

Per-venue access notes (CORS / geo, researched Jul 2026)

    Executable cross

    Line: net edge (best bid − best ask − 2×fee, as % of mid) over this session. Above zero = a candidate arbitrage at top-of-book size.

    Same-claim & capacity caveats. USD and USDT are not the same claim — a USDT venue is included only via your explicit toggle, and a persistent “edge” against it may be the USDT/USD rate, not free money. Top-of-book quotes say nothing about size: a real fill needs order-book depth, and cross-venue settlement needs inventory on both sides (see the strategy lab's five tests). Public endpoints are rate-limited — this tool stays far below the limits at the default interval.

    Opportunity log

    Every poll where the net cross was positive, with the venues and prices involved.

    Data: free public endpoints of Coinbase Exchange, Bitstamp, Kraken, Gemini (USD) and Binance, OKX (USDT), fetched directly by your browser — the strongest browser-verified pair is Coinbase + Bitstamp; venues without CORS or geo-blocked in your region simply show an error status and are excluded. Upgrade paths: CoinGecko's /coins/{id}/tickers (one free-demo-key call returning the same coin on dozens of exchanges, ~1–2 min cached) for a wide view, and Kalshi/Polymarket keyless read APIs for a prediction-market module. Educational tool, not investment advice. Part of web-apps.
