# Value Lab — can prediction beat the market?

The value approach (bet/trade when your predicted probability disagrees with the
market's price) examined across domains, with every figure linked to a source:

- **Scoreboard + evidence**: who wins, prediction or market, in sports betting,
  elections, geopolitical forecasting, equities, rates, agriculture, and
  weather/energy — from Kovalchik and SPIVA to the Atanasov et al. head-to-head
  where aggregated team forecasts beat a real prediction market.
- **Practitioner playbook**: closing line value, Brier-scored forecast logs,
  deflated Sharpe ratios, fractional Kelly, realistic magnitudes (3–7% ROI for
  verified bettors), and the documented wins that bought structural advantages
  (neighbor polls, satellite data, owning the flow).
- **Tool 1 — edge-proof calculator**: how many bets it takes to statistically
  distinguish a claimed edge from luck.
- **Tool 2 — humility blender**: market-as-prior, model-as-signal; blend in
  log-odds and see whether any bet survives the venue's actual payout.
